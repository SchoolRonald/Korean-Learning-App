About confidence

Confidence estimates how likely you are to spell the word correctly right now. It is accuracy times two kinds of decay. Accuracy is (correct + 1) / (attempts + 2), so a single lucky answer does not count as certain. Both decays count from your last correct answer: time decay halves every few days since then, and answer decay every few drill answers since then. Both half-lives double with each correct answer in a row (1 day or 10 answers after a miss, then 2 days or 20 answers, and so on, up to 64 days or 640 answers). So a wrong answer can only lower confidence. Words never answered correctly are 0%. Only spelling-drill answers count, not sentence grading. The spelling drill picks randomly from the 5 least confident words.
