    About confidence

    Confidence estimates how likely you are to spell the word correctly right now. It is accuracy times two kinds of decay. Accuracy is (correct + 1) / (attempts + 2), so a single lucky answer does not count as certain. Both decays count from your last correct answer: time decay halves every few days since then, and answer decay every few drill answers since then. Both half-lives double with each correct answer in a row (1 day or 10 answers after a miss, then 2 days or 20 answers, and so on, up to 64 days or 640 answers). So a wrong answer can only lower confidence. Words never answered correctly are 0%. Spelling-drill answers count, and so do Sentence answers: each learned word in a sentence gets its own right or wrong, and one sentence counts as one answer for the decay. Grammar points use the same formula, counting practice, review and Sentence answers about grammar. The spelling drill picks randomly from the 5 least confident words.
