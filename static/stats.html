About confidence

Confidence estimates how likely you are to spell the word correctly right now. It is accuracy times memory. Accuracy is (correct + 1) / (attempts + 2), so a single lucky answer does not count as certain. Memory halves every few days since you last saw the word, and that half-life doubles with each correct answer in a row (1 day after a miss, then 2, 4, 8, up to 64). Unpracticed words are 0%. Only spelling-drill answers count, not sentence grading. The spelling drill picks randomly from the 5 least confident words.
